About us

Technology works best when learning comes first.

TEKTAGOGY Group is an educational technology consultancy being developed to help educational institutions, organisations and educators use technology to strengthen teaching, learning and training.

We bring together technology and pedagogy to support the design, development and effective use of digital learning environments. Our services are shaped around each client's context, users and learning goals.

Connecting Technology with Pedagogy

We believe digital tools should serve clear teaching and learning objectives. That means considering the platform, the course design, the educator and the learner—not just the technology itself.
